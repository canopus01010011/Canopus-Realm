The story behind the musical, and the story behind this site.

The musical

Epic: The Musical is a song-cycle retelling of Homer's Odyssey, released saga by saga rather than all at once — each saga its own self-contained chapter of the ten-year voyage home from Troy.

Rather than a traditional stage script, it plays out as a concept album: every character gets their own voice and sound, and the songs carry the plot the way dialogue would in a script. It's built to be listened to in order, start to finish, the way you'd read a book chapter by chapter.

This site follows the first seven sagas and their thirty songs — from the fall of Troy to the edge of wisdom's saga — with more of the journey still to come.

This site

This is a fan-made project: no affiliation with the musical's creators, no claim to the music, art, or story. It exists because the source material is genuinely worth a proper home page — a place to meet the cast, follow the sagas in order, and listen through the whole thing without digging through a dozen tabs.
