Why log daily?

A single day of data barely tells a story — but seven days in a row starts to reveal your real patterns: sleep debt, hydration dips, and the walks that actually move the needle.

  • Steps — general activity, not just workouts.
  • Water — most people underestimate this by ~30%.
  • Sleep — the metric most tied to how the rest go.
  • Weight is optional — log it weekly, not daily, for a cleaner trend.
